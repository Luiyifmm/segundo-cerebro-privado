Stated by the user on 2026-10-09:

- **Trial reels ("vtrials")**: free to edit however I think is best.
- **Editing a video from scratch** (real client work): deliver an easy-to-edit, quality base — good cuts, good subtitles, motion-graphics ideas — so they can review and improve it. Subtitles and all material used from the original video must stay editable in Premiere.
- **ClienteA fonts**: SF Pro is his font; a second font (the Nyght Serif italic I used) is welcome for keywords.
- **Motion graphics**: the Pinterest/Instagram references are inspiration, not to copy literally; sometimes go simpler. This will be refined over time ("ya están un poco mejor, pero aún hay cosas que se pueden mejorar").

**Why:** they review and polish every video themselves, so anything baked into a rendered clip blocks their revision.

**How to apply** (my interpretation): raw clips cut on the timeline (not a rendered base), zooms as clip scale instead of baked in, images as their own image clips, subtitles via the MOGRT in `clientes/_plantillas/` (`Subtitulo ClienteA.mogrt` = SF Pro Bold + Nyght, see [[estilo-objetivo-sproject]]), motion graphics as separate clips on their own track. References saved in `~/Videos-Remotion/referencias/motion-graphics/` (poster look: cream paper or black backgrounds, B&W cutouts, grid, barcode and small technical labels, one accent color, sans + script/serif keyword). Trial vs brand context: [[clientes-y-carga-de-trabajo]].
