On 2026-10-09 the user shared (Telegram, saved locally) `~/Videos-Remotion/referencias/catalogo-formatos/00_catalogo_completo.mp4` (150 s, contact sheets in `hojas/`). It shows one ClienteA reel in a letterbox layout (black bars, two-line title on top with a highlighted word, video in the middle) varied 88 ways in 8 groups: Imagen y encuadre (12), Color y textura (8), Fondo y barras (7), Título y texto (22), Subtítulos (15), Tiempo y ritmo (11), Elementos extra (7), CTA (6).

Goal stated with it: find 10–20 professional formats, named by number (they prefer numbers), to re-upload the same video to Instagram Trials without it looking the same. The message also said the catalog contains things that break the clean, professional look and that the established rules must always be followed (see [[como-editar-para-el-usuario]], [[estilo-objetivo-sproject]]).

**Why:** recycle each video many times for Trials.
**How to apply:** I proposed a numbered list of 20 on 2026-10-09; it is NOT approved yet — check with the user before treating any number as fixed. My reading of what breaks the look (not confirmed): rotation, mirroring with text, colour tints, light leaks, code/grid backgrounds, green/purple gradient bars, fonts other than SF Pro, highlight colours other than blue, outlined or boxed text, capitals, emojis, pop/bounce animation, red circle and arrow, REC indicator, timer, invented news capture, yellow CTA pills.

User reply 2026-10-09: "Esto solo aplica para los v trials y los formatos anteriores que habías hecho también son buenas ideas." So the numbered formats are for Trials only (client edits keep the editable, clean workflow), and my earlier trial formats belong in the list.

Merged list I sent that day (v2, still awaiting explicit approval of the numbers): 1 Base con barras · 2 Pantalla completa · 3 Tarjeta redondeada · 4 Tarjeta con brillo azul · 5 Fondo desenfocado · 6 Azul noche · 7 Blanco y negro con azul · 8 Primer plano · 9 Descentrado · 10 Palabra a palabra · 11 Frase entera · 12 Portada + bucle · 13 Rápido (1,1×, cortes, punch-ins) · 14 Corto (15–20 s) · 15 Pantalla partida · 16 Estudio (ClienteA recortado sobre otro fondo) · 17 Miniaturas elegantes · 18 Póster (motion graphics a pantalla completa) · 19 Palabra gigante · 20 Fotos que caen. 15–20 already exist as Remotion versions in `motion/src/alternativas-datos.js` (versions 1–16); 1–14 need the letterbox base template built.

Strategy for the infoproductor (ClienteA), relayed 2026-10-09: every idea is always published twice — a normal version and a Trials version. When an idea wins, they ask for a "remix": I take that piece of content and deliver it edited in the 20 formats for Trials. Trigger phrase: "sácame las 20 alternativas" of a given piece. Hard rules that cannot be skipped: same font family always (two or three fonts at most) and the basic brand rules. They want a small test first to give editing corrections.
