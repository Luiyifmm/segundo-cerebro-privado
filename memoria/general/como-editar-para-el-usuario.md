Stated by the user on 2026-10-09:

- **Trial reels ("vtrials")**: free to edit however I think is best.
- **Editing a video from scratch** (real client work): deliver an easy-to-edit, quality base — good cuts, good subtitles, motion-graphics ideas — so they can review and improve it. Subtitles and all material used from the original video must stay editable in Premiere.
- **Amin fonts**: SF Pro is his font; a second font (the Nyght Serif italic I used) is welcome for keywords.
- **Motion graphics**: the Pinterest/Instagram references are inspiration, not to copy literally; sometimes go simpler. This will be refined over time ("ya están un poco mejor, pero aún hay cosas que se pueden mejorar").

**Why:** they review and polish every video themselves, so anything baked into a rendered clip blocks their revision.

**How to apply** (my interpretation): raw clips cut on the timeline (not a rendered base), zooms as clip scale instead of baked in, images as their own image clips, subtitles via the MOGRT in `clientes/_plantillas/` (`Subtitulo Amin.mogrt` = SF Pro Bold + Nyght, see [[estilo-objetivo-sproject]]), motion graphics as separate clips on their own track. References saved in `~/Videos-Remotion/referencias/motion-graphics/` (poster look: cream paper or black backgrounds, B&W cutouts, grid, barcode and small technical labels, one accent color, sans + script/serif keyword). Trial vs brand context: [[clientes-y-carga-de-trabajo]].

Corrections later the same day (2026-10-09), these supersede the notes above where they differ:
- Amin subtitles: everything in SF Pro (keyword too: SF Pro Bold, bigger, blue). The two-font look (SF Pro + Nyght serif italic) is only for motion graphics. Current template: `clientes/_plantillas/Subtitulo Amin v4.mogrt` (`ae/mogrt-amin-v4.jsx`; 30 s source, controls for entrance duration and keyword delay).
- Subtitle clips must be stretchable with the mouse on the timeline. After `seq.importMGT`, trim with BOTH `clip.end = fin` and then `clip.outPoint = fin - start`; setting only one leaves the source out point wrong and the clip cannot be extended (or ends up 30 s long). Swap script: `premiere/subs-v4.jsx`, repair: `premiere/reparar-recortes.jsx`.
- The user rearranges tracks while I work (subtitles moved to V5, nested sequence on V4, their images on V2/V3): always detect the subtitle track (the one with most MOGRT clips) and check a track is free before placing anything.
- Template sliders: Premiere clamps AE slider controls to 0–100 and editing `definition.json` min/max does not change it. So the current Amin template `Subtitulo Amin v6.mogrt` (`ae/mogrt-amin-v6.jsx`) maps them: "Tamano (50 = normal, 100 = doble)" and "Altura (50 = normal, mas = sube)". Swap script: `premiere/subs-v6.jsx`. Premiere also cannot edit template controls with several clips selected: copy one clip's settings to the rest with `premiere/igualar-subs.jsx`.
- Amin subtitle look, taken from the user's own example (first 4 s of AMIN_1009_01, 2026-10-09): highlight only in blue (never gray for the "bad" word), keyword ~2.4× the line size, white line sitting right on top of the keyword with almost no gap, both left-aligned as one compact block, placed on the chest. Template that does this in one clip: `clientes/_plantillas/Subtitulo Amin v8.mogrt` (`ae/mogrt-amin-v8.jsx`, swap script `premiere/subs-v8.jsx`; v8 adds shadow on/off, opacity, blur and distance controls); the user's working size is about 68 on its size control with Motion Y ≈ 0.43. When they say "like my example", read their clips first (`premiere/leer-subs.jsx`) and leave their example clips untouched.
- Sound (2026-10-09): the user had me study two sound-design videos and singled out what they liked: the same sound layered three times in different frequency ranges (low/mid/high), pitching sounds down to make them deeper or up to make them brighter, and carving the music's mid frequencies with an EQ so the voice sounds cleaner while someone speaks. Apply these by default. Details and my working rules: `~/.claude/skills/edicion-video/criterio.md` › Sonido. Layered/panned files are made with ffmpeg (`asetrate` copies aligned on their peaks) and kept per reel in `clientes/<c>/<reel>/sfx/`.
- 2026-10-09: the user confirmed the sound test worked ("ha mejorado el sonido la verdad"): fewer clicks, layered effects, panned shutters, riser into impact, music with a voice gap. Use that approach by default.
- 2026-10-09: motion graphics made with emojis (like the brain/abundance one in Amin's video) are NOT discarded: the user says that style works and they do not dislike it. Keep it as a valid option alongside the textured/poster style; my earlier note calling emojis a break of the clean look was my own reading and is withdrawn.
